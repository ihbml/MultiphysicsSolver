## 3. Caveats

1. **Native CAD Conversion Dependencies**: Execution of `step214ug.cmd` on `.prt` files requires an active Siemens NX license (either node-locked or reachable FlexLM license server). If the license server is unreachable on the host system, the bridge must invoke its graceful fallback to open standards without raising an unhandled exception.
2. **OpenCASCADE / PythonOCC Availability**: On Python 3.14 on Windows, `pythonocc-core` does not provide pre-built PyPI wheels. However, `gmsh` (4.15.2, which embeds OpenCASCADE OCCT internally), `trimesh` (5.1.1), `numpy` (2.5.4), and `scipy` (1.18.1) have verified wheels and provide full B-Rep parsing, Delaunay tessellation, and mesh topology auditing.
3. **Genus-0 Assumption**: The primary Euler criterion $\chi = 2$ applies to genus-0 simply connected bodies. For bodies with $g$ through-holes (e.g. pipe with internal bypass or heat exchanger tube banks), $\chi = 2 - 2g$. The auditor is specified to accept genus $g$ as a parameter while defaulting to $g=0$.
